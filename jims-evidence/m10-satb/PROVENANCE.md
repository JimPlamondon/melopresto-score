# JiMStaff Milestone 10 — song-wide/per-staff sensory evidence

This bundle is the automated visual evidence for the accepted Milestone 10 plan. It renders the shipped empty SATB JiMStaff template and the Old Hundredth four-part excerpt twice each, from fresh working directories, using the task's Qt 6.10.2 MuseScore bundle.

`render_evidence.sh` writes the repeated page PNGs and `renders/SHA256SUMS`. `verify_m10_satb.py` requires every hash to match, each repeated page to be byte-identical, every detected SATB system to retain four JiMStaff frames and broken vocal barlines, the four empty template extents to equal the Kernel-pinned Soprano, Alto, Tenor, and Bass defaults, and the rendered red Do row in every empty staff to land at the Kernel-pinned offset within its arbitrary extent. It also requires dark crescent ink immediately above and below every interior Do and a horizontal closure at both clipped frame edges. The exact renderer-level oracle independently counts every intersecting crescent, every adjoining Do horn, and every closure in `Engraving_JiMStaffM8BandElisionTests.m8DoRowsCarryRedLinesCrescentHornsAndDoDots`.

Renderer: `build.satb-centers.install/mscore.app`, MuseScore Studio Development 4.7.5, Qt 6.10.2 from `/Users/jim/Qt/6.10.2/macos`, with the bridge linked from `/Users/jim/Developer/JiMS/Temp/jims-codex-empty-satb-range-centers`. Command: `QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen mscore -r 120 -o page.png score.mscx`.

Visual scope is deliberate: Milestone 10 has no audio acceptance dimension. The final full-resolution inspection checks every clef for a continuous pair of crescent portions at its interior Do, horizontal closure at both clipped edges, and every page for stray accidental-class shaped noteheads; the diatonic Old Hundredth excerpt must use plain oval noteheads throughout.

Crescent-corrective execution result (2026-08-24): `render_evidence.sh` produced six PNGs. `verify_m10_satb.py` exited 0; all six SHA-256 entries verified; the three repeated pages were byte-identical; the pixel/layout checks passed on every page; the four template extents matched the uniform Kernel range-centred defaults; every detected Do row matched its expected 900¢, 200¢, 700¢, or 400¢ offset for Soprano, Alto, Tenor, or Bass; both adjoining crescent portions reached every interior Do; and both clipped frame edges carried closures. Full-resolution inspection of the three unique pages (the other three are byte-identical repeats) confirmed complete crescents and closures, four distinct empty-staff positions, and no stray shaped notehead; every Old Hundredth notehead is a plain oval. The installed app passed `codesign --verify --deep --strict` with `Authority=JiMS Dev`, CDHash `0c2fd17cfdab0683f1f4b429dbdb2118a499f8db`, and executable SHA-256 `51c7233155943b69c4fda1e54ec1198b9a705c8523881e900601eb15f35f3a2b`.

## Update, 2026-09-14: the boundary-Do contract replaces the range-centred one

The empty-staff assertions above describe the design of 2026-08-24, in which each empty staff was an octave centred on the Kernel's vocal default and Do sat inside the frame at 900, 200, 700 and 400 cents above the lower edge for Soprano, Alto, Tenor and Bass. On 2026-09-08 the owner ruled, and visually accepted (workstream `ws_staff_edges_20260908`; Kernel commit `0dac1fb`; fork PR 44, merged 2026-09-09), that an empty tonic-bounded staff starts on its tonic ratio row with only the upper edge expanded to the first ratio row at or above half a period. For the template's C Do-mode 12-TET staves that is a Do-to-So frame with the red Do line as the lower edge and the crescent's lower horn ending on it. The Kernel test `empty_tonic_bounded_frames_start_on_the_tonic_ratio` and the fork's engraving tests already pin that; this verifier had not been re-run since 2026-08-24 and still pinned the retired design.

`verify_m10_satb.py` now asserts, for every empty staff of every system: the red Do row lies on the frame's lower edge (within 2 px); the frame is 700 cents tall, converted at 100 cents per line distance and MuseScore's default spatium at 120 dpi (57.9 px, tolerance 3 px); crescent ink reaches Do from above and none continues below the Do edge; and the clipped upper edge carries a horizontal closure. The template-extent check against the Kernel-pinned vocal defaults is unchanged and still passes. The M9 verifier's system grouping and gap slack changes (see `../m9-satb/PROVENANCE.md`, update of 2026-09-14) apply here too, since this verifier imports it.

Negative checks run at the change: the retired 2026-08-24 renders are rejected (Do row not on the lower edge; frame 99 px instead of 57.9), and the Old Hundredth's written octave frames are rejected by the height check when pushed through the empty-staff path. Renderer, results and the regenerated renders are recorded in the verify summary and in the final report of workstream `ws_satb_template_verifiers_20260914`.
